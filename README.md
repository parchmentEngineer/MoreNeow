# MORE NEOW
Want to make the start of your runs more interesting? More Neow adds 12 brand new relics to Neow's pool to pick from at the start of each run. Enchant your cards, gain additional rest site options, and find new ways to play! Currently, the mod includes 8 options in Neow's positve pool, 3 options in her cursed pool, and one brand new option that doesn't fit in either pool - the Unfamiliar Deckbox. Inside the deckbox, each character gains a brand new starter deck, with fewer Strikes/Defends and more cards that synergize with their card pool in unique ways!

If you have any feedback about any of the relics (either how one performed in a run, or why you chose not to pick a particular relic), please let me know! I'm always open to hearing feedback and suggetions. Have fun!

## Changelog

v1.0.0: First full release (for Slay the Spire beta v0.105.1 / BaseLib v3.1.3), featuring:
* 8 positive relics: Blue-Tier Membership, Hunter's Guide, Inro, Jagged Coral, Picnic Basket, Shifting Blade, Wind Chimes, Wriggling Ink
* 3 cursed relics: Ancient Link, Iron Crown, Specter's Grin
* 1 special relic: Unfamiliar Deckbox
* 10 alternate starting cards: Combo Strike, Brawl, Blade Arc, Ready, Lens Flare, Reform, Nudge, Reinforce, Polish, Ooze

v1.0.1: Buffed several relics and most alternate starter decks.
* Added config toggles for "Always Offer a New Relic", "Always Offer Unfamiliar Deckbox", and "Only Offer New Relics".
* Buffed Iron Crown: Now has a downside of "Combats during Act 1 drop no gold" instead of "Card rewards during acts 2 and 3 have 2 fewer options".
* Buffed Wriggling Ink: Now enchants 2 cards with Steady instead of 1.
* Buffed Shifting Blade: Now lets you enchant a card of your choice with Unstable instead of adding an Unstable Strike.
* Buffed Blue Tier Membership: Now also causes shops to always stock at least 1 Rare potion.
* Made Picnic Basket get faded out when used.
* Nerfed Brawl: No longer upgrades to -1 energy cost, now gives 3 (7) Block when played instead of 4.
* Buffed Knife Arc: Now deals 5 (8) damage when played instead of 4 (7).
* Buffed Ready: No longer upgrades to Sly, now gives an extra 2 (5) Block when played.
* Buffed Lens Flare: Now only gives a random colorless attack instead of any random colorless card.
* Buffed Nudge: Now deals 6 (9) damage instead of 5 (8).
* Buffed Reinforce: Now Summons 3 (4) instead of 2 (3).
* Buffed Ooze: Now gives 7 (10) Block instead of 6 (9).
